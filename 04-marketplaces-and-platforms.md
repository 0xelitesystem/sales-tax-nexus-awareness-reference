# Marketplaces and platforms

When you sell through a marketplace, the marketplace often collects and remits sales tax on your behalf under marketplace facilitator rules. When you sell directly through your own store, that responsibility is generally yours.

## Marketplace facilitator rules

Many states have rules that make large marketplaces responsible for collecting and remitting sales tax on sales made through them. Under these rules, the marketplace acts as the facilitator and handles the tax for transactions on its platform. For a seller, this can mean the tax on marketplace sales is taken care of without the seller registering separately for those sales, though the details depend on the state and the platform.

## Direct sales are different

Sales you make directly, through your own website or checkout, are generally not covered by marketplace facilitator rules, because there is no facilitator in between. For direct sales, the responsibility to determine nexus, register, collect, and remit typically falls on you. A seller who uses both a marketplace and a direct store can have the marketplace handling one channel while the seller handles the other.

## Why the mix causes confusion

Sellers often assume that because a marketplace handles tax on its sales, all their sales are covered. They are not, if the seller also sells directly. The two channels can have different treatment, and direct sales can create obligations the seller is responsible for even while marketplace sales are handled. Knowing which of your sales are covered and which are not is essential to knowing where your own duties lie.

## Inventory in marketplace warehouses

A separate wrinkle is that storing inventory in a marketplace's fulfillment centers can create physical nexus in the states where those centers sit, independent of who collects the tax. So a marketplace might handle tax collection on sales while the seller still has nexus obligations created by inventory location. This is a common blind spot worth confirming with a professional if you use marketplace fulfillment.
