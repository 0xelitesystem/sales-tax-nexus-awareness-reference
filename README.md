# Sales Tax Nexus Awareness

A plain-language reference for solo operators and small sellers on sales tax nexus: what it is, how economic nexus changed the rules, why selling into a state can create a tax obligation there, and what questions to ask. Awareness, not a compliance plan.

**Not legal or tax advice.** Sales tax rules vary by state and change often. This explains the concepts so you know what to watch for and when to get help. It does not tell you whether you owe tax anywhere or how to file. Confirm your situation with a qualified tax professional.

## The core idea

Sales tax nexus is the connection between a seller and a state that is strong enough to require the seller to collect and remit that state's sales tax. Since the rules changed, nexus can be created purely by the volume of sales into a state, even with no physical presence there. This means an online seller can owe sales tax in states they have never set foot in, once they cross that state's threshold.

## Why awareness matters

Many small sellers do not know nexus exists until an obligation has already built up. The cost of awareness is low; the cost of ignorance can be back taxes and penalties. This reference is meant to put nexus on your radar early.
